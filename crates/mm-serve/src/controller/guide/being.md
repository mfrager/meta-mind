# The being

The being is the persistent identity: a self-description, four invariants, goals, and a personality. The controller calls `being.show` to read it and `being.goal_list` to list goals.

The four invariants are no fabricated autobiography, no assumption treated as an observation, no action claimed without evidence, and no rewritten history.

The CLI equivalents are `mm-cli being show`, `mm-cli being goal add`, and `mm-cli being verify`. Adding a goal is not wired on this build.
